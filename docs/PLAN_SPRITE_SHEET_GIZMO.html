A — the first-cell rectangle

A highlighted rectangle over grid cell 0. Dragging its body moves the grid origin (marginX/marginY); dragging its corner handle scales the cell (cellW/cellH). The NumberFields remain and stay in sync both ways.

Priority: handle → body → the existing cell-toggle / pan. A body press is provisional: it becomes a move only past a few pixels, so cell 0 does not become the one cell you can never click into the animation.

The schema: cell size becomes optionally EXPLICIT

cellW = max(1, floor((imgW − marginX − (cols−1)·spacingX) / cols)) — derived, and locked to the FULL image width, so a sheet with trailing dead space cannot be gridded at all today.
