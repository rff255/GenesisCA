2 · 3D View — lighting controls (light ball + ambient / diffuse / specular)

Before — one hardcoded light
The voxel + agent-sphere shaders bake one world-space directional light. No way to relight a dark face, flatten the shading, or make the volume pop.
After — Lighting block in the 3D View panel

Drag the bright dot on the ball to aim the light — the standard "sun position" widget. View anchors the light to the camera (shading stays put while orbiting — headlight/matcap style); World fixes it in the scene (orbiting sweeps the shadow side — sun style). Sliders: Ambient (base fill), Light (directional strength), Shine (white Blinn-Phong highlight, default 0). Defaults reproduce today's look exactly.

3 · 3D View — cell gaps toggle

Before — always-on gaps (cube scale 0.92)
After — "Cell gaps" checkbox (off = seamless volume)
Cell gaps
Alpha blend
Mirrors the 2D gridlines toggle. ON (default) keeps the historical lattice look; OFF renders adjacent cells flush (cube scale 1.001 — the hair of overlap avoids z-fighting on shared faces that exact 1.0 would cause). Picking uses the same scale, so clicks land with either setting.
